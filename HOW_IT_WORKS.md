# How the Energy Unit (EU) System Works
*(Plain English version)*

## The Big Idea

Most money today is just numbers that governments or banks create.  
This system tries something different: every digital unit is tied to **real electricity that was actually produced**.

**1 Energy Unit (EU) = 1 kilowatt-hour of verified electricity.**

Think of it like a digital receipt that proves a certain amount of real energy exists (or existed).

## The Rules (These Never Change)

1. **You can only create new EUs when real electricity is produced**  
   (or when a battery releases energy it previously stored).

2. **You can never have more EUs floating around than the total real electricity that has been verified.**  
   This is the most important rule. It keeps the system honest.

3. **When someone uses the electricity, they “redeem” (burn) the EUs.**  
   The digital claim disappears because the real energy was delivered.

4. **Batteries can move energy through time, but they always lose some energy in the process.**  
   The system accounts for that loss honestly. It never pretends storage creates free energy.

## Simple Example

- A solar farm produces 10,000 kWh of electricity on a sunny day.  
  → 10,000 new EUs are created.

- A small fee (1%) goes into a shared “Treasury.”  
  → The solar farm gets 9,900 EUs.

- The solar farm sells some of those EUs to a factory and a data center.

- Later, the factory and data center use the electricity and redeem (burn) the EUs.

- At every moment, the total number of EUs still in circulation is less than or equal to the total electricity that has been verified.

## Why This Matters

- It ties digital value to something physical and useful (energy).
- It makes it harder to create “money” out of thin air.
- It can work alongside regular money or other systems.
- The same idea could later be connected to real electricity meters and batteries.

## Current Status

This is a working **simulation** (a computer model).  
It is not yet connected to real power plants or a live blockchain.  
It is a clear, honest foundation that can be improved and eventually connected to the real world.
